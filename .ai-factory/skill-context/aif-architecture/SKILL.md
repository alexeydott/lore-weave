**Source**: codebase convention
**Rule**: Architecture proposals assign Rust to kernel-derived services, Go to domain/meta, Python to AI/LLM, and TypeScript to gateway/BFF/realtime unless the authoritative language contract is deliberately amended with its enforcement.

### Design for Multi-Tenant Self-Hosting
**Source**: codebase convention
**Rule**: Self-hosted never means single-user or local-PC-only. Design for multiple users and devices sharing infrastructure with explicit System, Per-user, and Per-book ownership tiers.

### Preserve Gateway and MCP Boundaries
**Source**: codebase convention
**Rule**: Keep `api-gateway-bff` as the public HTTP edge, the sanctioned `game-server` WebSocket exception, `ai-gateway` as MCP federation, and domain services as owners of agent tools.

### Preserve Provider Ownership
**Source**: codebase convention
**Rule**: `provider-registry-service` is the only home for provider SDKs, credentials, model discovery, pricing, and local backend registration; architecture must not distribute these concerns.

### Preserve Per-Service Data Ownership
**Source**: codebase convention
**Rule**: Each microservice owns its PostgreSQL database. Integrate through contracts/events, keep Redis ephemeral, keep glossary authored truth separate from derived knowledge data, and forbid shared-table shortcuts.

### Avoid Platform Lock-In
**Source**: codebase convention
**Rule**: Target portable Docker, PostgreSQL, and S3-compatible infrastructure across AWS, other clouds, VPS, or homelab; do not make Vercel- or Cloudflare-specific services architectural requirements.
